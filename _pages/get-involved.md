The DynamiCog Lab is **actively recruiting**. If you're interested in the research we do
and want to get involved, please read below and reach out.

## Undergraduate Research Assistants

We regularly bring on undergraduate RAs to help run studies, work with behavioral and
language data, and contribute to ongoing projects. No specific prior experience is
required — curiosity, reliability, and interest in the science matter most.

- Fill out the **[research interest survey](#)** <!-- paste your current survey link -->
- Email Nick at **nicholas.duran [at] utexas.edu** to introduce yourself.

## Prospective Graduate Students

Students interested in joining the lab typically apply through the
[UT Austin Department of Psychology](https://liberalarts.utexas.edu/psychology/) graduate
program. If our research is a good fit for your interests, email Nick before you apply so
we can talk about goals and fit.

## What we look for

Our work spans cognitive dynamics, communication, computational linguistics, and
multimodal behavior. Comfort with (or willingness to learn) programming in R or Python is
a plus for many projects, but we support students in building those skills.
