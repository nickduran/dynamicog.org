In collaboration with many talented researchers, the DynamiCog Lab has co-created several
community resources, freely available below.

## ALIGN
A Python library for extracting quantitative, reproducible metrics of multi-level
alignment between two speakers in naturalistic language corpora.
[GitHub](https://github.com/nickduran/align-linguistic-alignment) ·
[OSF](https://osf.io/ur543/) ·
[Paper (Duran, Fusaroli, & Paxton, 2019)](/publications/)

## Mousetrack
An R package that extracts dynamical measures of arm-reaching movements from
two-dimensional x–y trajectory coordinates.

## Devil's Advocate Corpus
Conversational transcripts of truthful and deceptive speech on controversial topics
(Central California, 2012). Distributed via the Inter-university Consortium for Political
and Social Research (ICPSR), Ann Arbor, MI.
