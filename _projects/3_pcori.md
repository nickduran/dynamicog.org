Funded by PCORI (Role: PI). This project applies computational linguistics and machine
learning — including large language models — to the analysis of secure messaging between
patients and providers, aiming to enhance patient decision-making effectiveness. By
integrating LLMs with traditional machine learning methods, we work to better identify
effective deliberations in clinical exchanges, deepen insights from data analytics, and
improve the translation of comparative effectiveness research into clinical practice.
