Part of an NSF center effort on human–robot co-adaptation, bringing together
collaborators across computer science and psychology at UT Austin (with Joydeep Biswas,
Roberto Martín-Martín, Justin Hart, and Chen Yu). The lab contributes expertise on the
behavioral dynamics of coordination and communication — how people converge, adapt, and
build shared understanding — to the design of robots that co-adapt with the humans they
work alongside.
