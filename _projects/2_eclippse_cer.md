This work uses advanced computational linguistics to analyze secure messaging between
patients and providers, with the goal of enhancing patient-centered outcomes. Secure
messaging is a common form of clinical communication and offers rich linguistic data for
improving patient decision-making. We leverage large language models for predictive
analysis, integrating them with traditional machine learning to better identify effective
deliberations in clinical exchanges — helping health systems employ AI to support
effective clinical communication.

A UCSF/Kaiser collaboration (with Wagahta Semere and colleagues). Builds on the ECLIPPSE
study of physician–patient linguistic concordance across ~5,000 patients and ~1,100
physicians. See related work in *Science Advances* (2021) and the *Journal of Biomedical
Informatics* (2021).
